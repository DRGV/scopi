PROBLEM found, and what I changed (all on the Mac, none tested on the Pi yet):
1. The Preview box did nothing, for every filter, not only the Gaussian slider. The app connected the dialog to the canvas only "if the Preview box is visible",
   asked before the dialog was on screen, where the answer is always no. Your test of typing a value and pressing Enter showed nothing about previews either:
   Enter presses OK, so that was the result being applied. Fixed, and the Mac tests now open the real dialog and check the pixels on the canvas.
2. The same mistake, the other way round, made "Process entire stack" always off. Fixed; this test is the first time it can be tried on the Pi.
3. The Gaussian slider was only 84 pixels wide (1 pixel of mouse travel moved Sigma by about 0.8). Sliders in the parameter dialogs are now at least 260 pixels wide.
4. A horizontal line's Angle was shown as -0 in the Results table (-0.0 in the CSV). It reads 0 now.
No camera is needed this round.

PART C - Process entire stack (about 5 minutes)
-----------------------------------------------

The file has 3 frames of random noise, each with its own average brightness: about 80, 128 and 175, StdDev about 40. A Gaussian blur of Sigma 3 takes the StdDev
down to about 4 and leaves the average alone, so Analyze > Measure tells you for each frame whether it was filtered.

C1. File > Open ~/imagepi-test/cells_stack.tif. A frame slider with "1/3" appears under the picture. Analyze > Show Results.
    Analyze > Measure (Ctrl+M) with frame 1, drag the frame slider to 2 and press Ctrl+M, to 3 and press Ctrl+M. Three rows, Mean about 80, 128 and 175, StdDev about 40 each.
    Go back to frame 1.
C2. Only the current frame. Process > Filters > Gaussian Blur..., Sigma 3, Process entire stack UNTICKED (the box is there because the image has 3 frames), OK.
    Measure frame 1, 2 and 3 again (Ctrl+M on each). Expect StdDev about 4 on frame 1 and about 40 on frames 2 and 3. Then Edit > Undo (Ctrl+Z), and go back to frame 1.
C3. The whole stack. Process > Filters > Gaussian Blur..., Sigma 3, tick Process entire stack, OK. Measure frame 1, 2 and 3 again.
    Expect StdDev about 4 on all three frames and the Mean unchanged. (Before this fix the box was ignored.)
    In the Results panel click "Save CSV...", and send me the file's text (9 rows, the Frame column says which frame each row is). Close the image (Ctrl+W).
