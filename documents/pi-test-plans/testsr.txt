PROBLEMS found, and what I changed (all on the Mac, none tested on the Pi yet):
1. Low fps (7.9 with a bare stage, 2.9 with the tissue-paper slide, the same at every resolution and with the crop). Not USB: the camera
   starts in AUTO EXPOSURE with a 350 ms limit, and 1 / 0.35 s = 2.9 fps. Your snap's Info says exposure_us 350000. The camera
   also picked about 130 ms on the bare stage (7.6 fps) in the command-line runs. Changes:
   - Scopi now limits the auto exposure time: a new "Auto limit" box in the Acquisition group, default 100 ms, remembered per camera.
   - The live view overlay now shows the exposure beside the fps ("1228 x 922   9.8 fps   exposure 100 ms (auto)").
   - pi_bringup.py camera now measures with a fixed 10 ms exposure by default (options --exposure-ms and --auto-limit-ms);
     camdiag has --exposure-ms. Every line says which exposure was used.
2. The live image is mirrored (you wrote "mirrored vertically (across y axis)", which can mean left-right or top-bottom). The SDK
   delivers the normal orientation, so this is most likely the microscope optics. New: "Flip horizontally" and "Flip vertically"
   checkboxes (Orientation row). They apply to the live view and to snaps and are remembered for this camera.
3. The Exposure box did not look greyed out when it was disabled, and neither did Bit depth (so "16-bit could not be set, clicking had
   no effect"). The dark theme had no colours for disabled controls: fixed. The camera has RAW8 only, i.e. no 16-bit mode, so the
   Bit depth box now says "8-bit only" (greyed, with a tooltip). Test3 C7 (16-bit snap) is therefore retired for this camera.
4. Every click on Refresh wrote seven "VIDEOIO ... can't open camera by index" lines to the log (OpenCV probing for webcams that do not
   exist). It now only probes /dev/video* nodes that exist, and silences OpenCV's own log while probing.
5. Small crop fixes: a second crop drawn on an already cropped picture is now added to the first crop's offset (before, it was wrong),
   and changing the resolution returns to the full frame.
6. Round 3 did not report Part D (outlines, summary table, undo, Gaussian slider, 18 MP save, speed). It is repeated below as Part D.
Not changed: test3 Part F (works on the command line but not in the app) and Part G (E_BUSY) no longer apply because the camera runs in
both. Skip them unless a start error comes back. Part F's one useful check, "the app leaves the camera free when it quits", is C8 below.
